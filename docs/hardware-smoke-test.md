# Hardware smoke test (manual)

CI and `pytest` never touch a camera, microphone, window or model inference in the app. Run this on the target Mac after changes to capture, gestures, rendering, recording or live follow, and note the result in `docs/implementation-progress.md`.

Setup: the lock-file environment, the models downloaded, and Camera and Microphone allowed for the terminal.

First run the automatic part, `.venv/bin/python scripts/hardware_check.py`. It covers the camera rate, the microphone and its device clock, a loopback click, the live model's load time and `say`, and needs no one at the Mac. The steps below are the ones that need you.

1. **Start.** Run `.venv/bin/python main.py samples/sample_notes.md`. The window shows the mirrored camera at ~30 fps (stats line, bottom right) with the notes on the left.
2. **Prepare gestures.** The tutorial card walks through the first gestures (Enter skips). Browse with one finger, two fingers and a flat hand, and focus. Open palm on a focused word: the ring shows the word, **stress** (or **unstress**) and **hear it**. Make an L and turn it like a knob: the ring turns, one node per ~15°, clockwise when the index tilts right, the picked node at 12 o'clock; turning back past the start wraps round. Turn to **hear it** and pinch + lift: `say` speaks the sentence. Turn to **stress** and pinch + lift: the word gets `*…*`. Press `u`: it's undone. A tone commit says it needs the optional LLM and that nothing was sent.
3. **Take.** Raise a fist and hold 1 s; the count-in runs and REC starts. Read the first section aloud: the orange sentence follows you, and the next section appears faint during the last sentence and takes over once you say its opening. Flick in the zone; the section moves on. Hold an open palm to stop.
4. **Review.** "TRANSCRIBING" appears, then the counts and pace. Chips show ✓ ✗ ? –. Focus a long sentence: its panel pages through every verdict line. Hold an open palm on the focused sentence (or press `a`): you hear it. After two takes, a focused sentence shows its takes as chips beside it: make an L and point at another chip, and the verdicts change to that take's. Pinch + lift: a drill of just that sentence.
5. **Keys.** Press `t`, speak, `n`, `b`, `x`. A take is recorded with no hand in view; in `session.json`, its `sections` show `start`, `key`, `key`.
6. **Recovery.** Start a take, speak ~5 s, and press Ctrl-C in the terminal. The take is kept as `interrupted`. Then start a take and kill the process (`kill -9 <pid>`). On the next start, a `recovered:` line appears for it.
7. **Clock.** Clap once at the start of a take. In `take-NN.transcript.json` and the gesture log, the clap should line up with `t_start` plus its time in the WAV to within ~50 ms. Note the offset you measure.
8. **Close.** Quit with `q`; the terminal says what analysis, if any, was left for later.
9. **Reopen.** Run `.venv/bin/python main.py --open <session folder name>`. It opens in Review with the takes' verdicts; `a` plays a sentence; a new take joins the same session.
10. **Export.** Run `.venv/bin/python -m palmcards.export <session folder name>`. A new file appears in the session's `exports/`, with your stress edit in it.
11. **Cloud LLM (optional; costs a little, sends the text you ask about to Anthropic).** With `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` in `.env` or the environment, run `.venv/bin/python main.py --llm anthropic`. The terminal names the model and where the key came from (never the key), and a CLOUD LLM chip shows at the bottom left. Open the ring on a focused word: the chip reads SENDING, the word scrambles, and alternatives join the ring (if you are already turning, the pick stays where it is); make an L and turn to one: it scrambles into the sentence and the label in orange, its box in the ring empty for a moment; turn back to the original, then to the alternative again, pinch + lift, then `u`. `grep ring_step` in the session's gesture log lists every step. Tilt the tone dial on a focused sentence and pinch + lift: a proposal arrives; focus it again and pinch + lift uses it. Open your palm on a focused sentence: suggested marks appear faded in it, the first outlined; make an L, then point to move the outline to a mark, pinch (no lift) to accept it (it turns solid; the pinch acts on the mark pointed at before the curl); pinch + lift adds just the accepted ones, `u` undoes. With Wi-Fi off, open the ring on another word: "ALTERNATIVES FAILED: NO CONNECTION, NOTHING CHANGED". Quit, then `.venv/bin/python -m palmcards.llm usage <session folder name>` lists the calls and their tokens.

Record: date, macOS version, which steps passed, the frame rate during a take, and anything odd.
