# Opening

Good evening, everyone. / Thank you for *being* here tonight.
We started this project with one question: // what if practice felt like a conversation? [rise]
[slow] Most of us rehearse in our heads, and our heads are very forgiving.

# The idea

So we built a mirror that *listens*. / It shows your notes beside your face.
[fast] You mark how each line should sound, you say it out loud, and it tells you what landed.
That's it. // No scores, no accent coaching, just your own plan checked against your own voice. [fall]
