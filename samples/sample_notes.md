# Opening

Good evening, everyone. Thank you for being here tonight.
We started this project with one question: what if practice felt like a conversation?
Most of us rehearse in our heads, and our heads are very forgiving.

# The idea

So we built a mirror that listens. It shows your notes beside your face.
You say each line out loud, and it shows you how the take went.
That's it. No scores, no accent coaching, just your own takes side by side.
